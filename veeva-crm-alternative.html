Compare / Veeva CRM alternative

A lightweight Veeva CRM alternative, for the targeting part only

Veeva CRM (now Vault CRM) is the industry-standard commercial platform for life sciences, and if you need field-force workflows, validated compliance tooling and Veeva OpenData’s verified HCP universe, it’s the right tool. But many teams adopt, or inherit, an enterprise CRM when the job in front of them is much smaller: turn HCP lists into scored, compliant, exportable audiences. That’s the entire job LiteHCP Targeter does.

Use LiteHCP Targeter for

Turning HCP lists into scored audiences with suppression applied, then exporting clean target lists into whatever you run.

Keep Veeva for

Field-force workflows, validated compliance tooling and OpenData’s verified HCP universe.

Planning the move to Vault CRM?

Vault CRM, on Veeva’s own platform, replaces the Salesforce-based Veeva CRM, and support for the Salesforce-based version ends at the end of December 2029. Keeping the targeting step outside the CRM means your lists survive the migration.

Target lists travel as plain CSVs

LiteHCP’s CRM target list (HCP ID, NPI, tier, planned calls per cycle) loads into the old CRM today and Vault CRM tomorrow without rebuilding audiences.

Who switches, or starts, with LiteHCP

  • Agencies and consultancies building target lists for multiple pharma clients without reselling enterprise seats.
  • Regional affiliates and small market teams whose HQ owns the CRM but who still need local targeting discipline.
  • Early-stage and specialty companies that aren’t ready for a CRM implementation but need defensible targeting now.
  • Data-residency-sensitive teams that want HCP data on their own infrastructure, not a vendor cloud.

Deploy in minutes

  1. Upload one filePut litehcp.php on any PHP 8 host. The SQLite database creates itself.
  2. RegisterThe first account becomes the admin. No email server or API keys needed.
  3. Load the sample200 scored HCPs, 9 enrichment rules and 6 audiences, ready to explore.

Your own CSVs import the same way: columns are matched for you and every row is checked before anything is written. Deployment guide

What you give up

Honesty matters here: LiteHCP has no verified HCP database, no field-force tooling, no omnichannel execution and no vendor compliance certifications. It produces clean, audited audience exports that feed whatever execution tools you already use. If your organization requires a validated enterprise stack end to end, use the enterprise stack.
