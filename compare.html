Honest comparison, updated November 2026

You probably don't need an enterprise suite to build a target list.

Platforms like Veeva Vault CRM, Salesforce Life Sciences Cloud and IQVIA OCE are excellent at what they do: running the entire commercial operation of a life-sciences company.

LiteHCP Targeter does one slice of that job: turning HCP CSVs into scored, compliant, exportable target lists. If that slice is all you need, the difference in cost and complexity is enormous.

The CRM market is moving

Many life-sciences teams are in the middle of a CRM migration. Keeping the targeting step outside the CRM means your lists survive whichever platform you land on.

Veeva

Vault CRM, on Veeva's own platform, replaces the Salesforce-based Veeva CRM. Support for the Salesforce-based version now ends at the end of December 2029.

Source: IntuitionLabs

Salesforce and IQVIA

In 2024 IQVIA agreed to license its OCE CRM software to Salesforce and collaborate on Life Sciences Cloud for Customer Engagement, which Salesforce has since launched. IQVIA continues to support existing OCE customers.

Source: Salesforce IntuitionLabs on IQVIA

What it means for targeting

LiteHCP's CRM target list (HCP ID, NPI, tier, planned calls per cycle) is a plain CSV, so the same audiences load into Vault CRM, Life Sciences Cloud or OCE without rebuilding them.

Which one is right for you?

Most teams that build target lists from CSVs don't need a platform to do it. Some do.

Choose LiteHCP Targeter

You need clean target lists, not a platform.

  • Your HCP data arrives as CSVs from conferences, partners, data vendors, or your own systems.
  • You're a small team, agency, or regional affiliate without enterprise CRM budget or admins.
  • Data residency matters: you want HCP data on infrastructure you control.
  • You want export discipline: suppression always applied, minimal fields by default, PII gated and audited.
  • You need it working this week, on hosting you already pay for.

Choose an enterprise platform

You're running the whole commercial engine.

  • You need verified HCP reference data (Veeva OpenData, IQVIA OneKey) kept current at scale.
  • Field reps need call planning, sampling, and territory management.
  • You execute email and omnichannel campaigns inside the same system.
  • You have compliance requirements that mandate a specific validated vendor stack.
  • You have the budget, admins, and implementation timeline to match.
